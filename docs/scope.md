# First-release scope

Users: workspace administrator, accountant, and approver.

Workflow:
1. Record suppliers, purchase orders, and received deliveries.
2. Upload a single-page invoice.
3. Extract fields and line items with source evidence.
4. Review and correct extracted values.
5. Check totals, duplicates, prices, and received quantities.
6. Resolve discrepancies and submit for independent approval.
7. Export an approved revision and retain its activity history.

Limits: English, EUR, one purchase order per invoice, simple line items,
one designated approver per workspace, PDF/JPEG/PNG up to 5 MB.

No bank transfers. Only synthetic data for the public demonstration.
An edited approved invoice requires new approval before export.

Acceptance example: 100 monitors ordered at EUR 200 each, 90 received,
100 invoiced at EUR 220 each. Flag the price and delivery discrepancies.
